# Background generation prompts

The final posters use generated, text-free backgrounds plus deterministic SVG typography. This keeps Chinese copy, CLI commands, and provider caveats exact.

## 01 — Login first

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-free background artwork for an X/Twitter promotional poster, final crop 16:9
Primary request: a premium cinematic visual expressing "use the coding-agent login you already have to generate images": a dark obsidian terminal-like environment transitioning into a vivid generated alpine sunrise landscape
Scene/backdrop: nearly black matte space on the left with subtle terminal grid and a single warm orange command-line beam flowing into a detailed mountain sunrise on the right
Subject: abstract transformation from a typed prompt into a beautiful generated image; no people
Style/medium: refined cinematic 3D editorial key art, sophisticated open-source developer-tool campaign, crisp and believable
Composition/framing: wide landscape; keep the left 48 percent dark, calm and uncluttered for typography; visual focus on the right; strong curved transition
Lighting/mood: warm sunrise glow against deep black, confident and premium
Color palette: obsidian black, warm orange, cream, muted forest green
Constraints: absolutely no text, no letters, no logos, no interface labels, no watermarks; no recognizable third-party marks; strong negative space for later typesetting
Avoid: cyberpunk neon overload, generic blue SaaS gradients, people, devices, mockup frames
```
